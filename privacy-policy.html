Zony Sports Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

Zony Sports is a sports tournament and community app. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use the app, we may collect profile photos, community posts, sports board posts, and direct messages you send to other users.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide app functionality. This includes authenticating your account, displaying your profile, enabling tournament registration, and allowing you to communicate with other users. We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

Third Party Services

Zony Sports uses Firebase by Google for authentication, data storage, and file storage. We also use Sentry for crash reporting to help us fix bugs and improve the app. These services may collect limited technical data as described in their own privacy policies.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Firebase. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from within the app, which will remove your personal data.

Children

Zony Sports is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this URL.
